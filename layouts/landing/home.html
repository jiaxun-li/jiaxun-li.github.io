I am a Statistics PhD student at the University of Michigan, advised by Ambuj Tewari and Saptarshi Chakraborty. I study the theoretical foundations of generative learning and the statistical principles behind reliable machine learning evaluation.

My work connects mathematical characterizations of generation with benchmark design, Python experiments, and finite-sample statistical inference.
